# Delete Your ScriptMate Account & Data

Last updated: 2026

ScriptMate takes your privacy seriously. This page explains how to request
deletion of your ScriptMate account and the personal data associated with
it.

---

## How to request deletion

Send an email to **[support@scriptmate.app](mailto:support@scriptmate.app?subject=ScriptMate%20Account%20Deletion%20Request)**
from the email address you used to sign in to ScriptMate (or from any
address if you have only used the app as an anonymous device user — see
below).

Please include the following in your email so we can locate and remove the
correct account:

- The subject line **"ScriptMate Account Deletion Request"**
- The email address associated with your ScriptMate account, if you signed
  in with Google or Apple
- If you only used ScriptMate as a device-anonymous user (no sign-in),
  please include the Device ID shown in the app under **Settings →
  Support → Diagnostics** so we can locate the right data
- A one-line confirmation that you want all your account and personal
  data permanently deleted

---

## What will be deleted

On receiving a valid deletion request, we permanently delete the following
server-side data tied to your account or device:

- Your **user profile** (email, name if provided, device ID, subscription
  state)
- All **scripts** you have uploaded or created
- All **rehearsal recordings and metadata** stored on our servers
- All **daily drill records**, line scores, and practice-time history
- All **authentication sessions and tokens**
- Your **RevenueCat subscriber linkage** on our side (your Google Play /
  App Store purchase record itself is managed by the store and must be
  cancelled or refunded separately through Google Play / Apple as
  applicable)
- Any **support messages or bug reports** you have submitted that are
  identifiable to you

**On-device data** (scripts and rehearsals cached locally on your phone or
tablet, local settings, offline cache) is removed when you uninstall the
app or use your device's "Clear app data" option — we have no technical
way to reach data stored only on your device.

---

## Data that may be retained after deletion

In accordance with our legal obligations and legitimate interests, a very
small amount of information may be retained in a non-identifying form
after deletion of your account:

- **Anonymised, aggregate text-to-speech usage counts** (characters per
  day, voice/model distribution). This ledger is used only for billing
  reconciliation with our voice-synthesis provider and for detecting
  abusive usage patterns. It contains **no script text and no personally
  identifying information** — only a hashed or disassociated user
  reference, dates, and totals.
- **Payment/subscription records** retained by Google Play (Android) or
  Apple (iOS) are governed by their respective privacy policies. ScriptMate
  does not store your payment card data at any time; those records are
  only visible to us as the subscription state exposed by RevenueCat.
- **Minimal legal/compliance records** such as the fact that a deletion
  request was received and completed (date and anonymised reference),
  retained to demonstrate compliance with data-protection law.

Nothing in this retention category can be used to re-identify you or
restore your deleted account.

---

## Processing time

We will complete your deletion request within **30 days** of receiving a
valid request, in line with GDPR / UK GDPR Article 12(3). In most cases
deletion is completed within a few business days.

You will receive an email confirmation from `support@scriptmate.app` once
your data has been removed.

---

## Cancelling your subscription

Deleting your account does **not** automatically cancel an active Google
Play or Apple App Store subscription. Please cancel through the store
directly:

- **Google Play (Android)** — [Manage your subscriptions on Google
  Play](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions)
- **Apple App Store (iOS)** — Settings → `[your name]` → Subscriptions →
  ScriptMate → Cancel Subscription

If your subscription is still active when you request deletion, we will
also remove our link to your RevenueCat subscriber record. The subscription
with the store itself must be cancelled by you through the links above.
