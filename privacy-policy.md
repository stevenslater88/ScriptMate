# ScriptM8 Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 14 October 2026**

ScriptM8 ("we", "us" or "our") is an acting rehearsal application designed to help actors learn scripts, practise lines, work with voice-based rehearsal tools and prepare self-tapes.

We take privacy seriously. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use ScriptM8, how it is used, which service providers may process it, how long it is retained and how you can exercise your privacy rights.

By using ScriptM8, you acknowledge that your information will be handled as described in this policy.

## 1. Who is responsible for your information?

ScriptM8 is the service responsible for the personal information processed through the application.

**Privacy and data deletion contact:** support@scriptmate.app

Please contact us using this email address if you have questions about this policy, want to exercise your privacy rights or wish to request deletion of your information.

## 2. Information we may collect

The information processed depends on which ScriptM8 features you use and whether you create an account.

### Account and authentication information

If you register or sign in, we may process information such as:

- Your email address.
- Your name or profile information, where provided by you or your sign-in provider.
- An account identifier and authentication-related information.
- Your subscription and entitlement status.

If you sign in using Google or Apple, those providers may process information under their own privacy policies. We receive the information made available to us through the selected authentication method.

### Scripts and rehearsal content

When you import, create, save or rehearse a script, ScriptM8 may process:

- Script text and dialogue.
- Character names and character assignments.
- Script titles and related metadata.
- Text extracted from imported PDF documents.
- Rehearsal settings and information needed to provide the selected features.

Scripts may contain personal information if you choose to include it. Please only upload material you are entitled to use and avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information.

### Voice generation and audio

If you use voice-generation or rehearsal features, we may process the text required to generate spoken dialogue, voice selections and related generation information.

ScriptM8 uses third-party voice-generation services, including ElevenLabs, for relevant features. Text and technical information required for a generation request may be sent to the applicable provider. That provider processes the information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Your use of a voice feature may therefore involve processing outside ScriptM8's own systems.

### Recordings and self-tapes

If you use self-tape or recording features, the application may access your camera and microphone after you grant permission.

Video and audio recordings may be created and saved to your device or exported to your device's media library. Server-side rehearsal recordings or associated metadata may also be processed where the relevant feature supports this.

We do not claim that all recordings are uploaded to our servers. The handling of a particular recording depends on the feature you use and where that feature stores the resulting data.

### Subscription and purchase information

ScriptM8 offers paid subscription features. Subscription services may process:

- Subscription status and entitlement information.
- Product identifiers and purchase or renewal status.
- Information needed to restore purchases or verify access to paid features.

We use RevenueCat to help manage and verify subscription entitlements. Purchases made through Google Play or Apple's App Store are also subject to the applicable store's privacy policy and terms.

We do not need your full payment card details to verify your ScriptM8 subscription.

### Technical and diagnostic information

To operate, maintain and troubleshoot the application, we may process technical information such as:

- Device or installation identifiers.
- Application version and operating system information.
- Error reports, crash information and diagnostic events.
- Information needed to investigate support requests and technical problems.

Some diagnostic information may be processed by third-party monitoring services, such as Sentry, where enabled.

## 3. How we use information

We use information for the following purposes:

- To create and manage accounts and authenticate users.
- To import, parse, save and retrieve scripts.
- To provide rehearsal, line-learning, teleprompter and self-tape features.
- To generate voices and deliver requested audio features.
- To verify subscriptions and provide paid features.
- To respond to support requests and account deletion requests.
- To diagnose errors, maintain security and improve reliability.
- To comply with applicable legal obligations and protect our legitimate rights.

We do not use your script content for unrelated purposes merely because you upload it to ScriptM8.

## 4. Legal bases for processing

Where the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) applies, we rely on one or more of these legal bases, as appropriate:

- **Contract:** processing necessary to provide the features or services you request.
- **Legitimate interests:** maintaining application security, preventing misuse, troubleshooting and improving reliability, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
- **Consent:** where consent is required, including for applicable device permissions or optional processing.
- **Legal obligation:** processing required to comply with applicable law.

Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent where applicable. Withdrawing consent does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful.

## 5. Permissions on your device

ScriptM8 may request access to device features when you use functions that need them.

- **Camera:** for recording self-tapes.
- **Microphone:** for recording audio or using relevant voice features.
- **Media library:** where required to save or export a completed recording.

You can manage permissions through your device settings. Denying a permission may prevent the associated feature from working.

PDF imports use the relevant document-selection functionality, allowing you to choose a document rather than granting unrestricted access to your files.

## 6. Third-party service providers

ScriptM8 may rely on service providers to operate its features. Depending on your use of the application, these may include:

- **ElevenLabs:** voice generation and related audio functionality.
- **RevenueCat:** subscription management and entitlement verification.
- **Google Play and Apple:** app distribution, authentication or payment-related services where applicable.
- **Backend hosting providers:** account management, script storage, API requests and other application services.
- **Sentry or other configured diagnostic services:** error reporting and application reliability.

These providers may process information necessary to deliver their services. Their own privacy notices explain their independent processing practices.

We do not sell your personal information to data brokers.

## 7. How we share information

We share information only where necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including:

- With service providers that help us operate ScriptM8.
- With authentication or app-store providers when you choose their services.
- Where required by law, a valid legal process or a competent authority.
- Where necessary to protect users, prevent fraud or address security issues.
- In connection with a business transfer, if permitted by applicable law and appropriate safeguards are in place.

We do not make your scripts publicly available simply because you store them in ScriptM8. You remain responsible for choosing whether and with whom to share any material you export or distribute.
